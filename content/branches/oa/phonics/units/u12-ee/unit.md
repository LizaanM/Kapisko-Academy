# One sound, many spellings

This week we learn the /ee/ sound and the four ways to write it. One sound,
four graphemes — and a "vowel team" that children meet everywhere.

## Lessons

1. **Monday** — Meet the sound. Grapheme `ee`.
2. **Tuesday** — A different spelling: `ea`.
3. **Wednesday** — The magic `e_e`.
4. **Thursday** — Mix it up: sort the spellings you know.
5. **Friday** — Show what you know: quiz + review.

## Variants

| Phoneme | Grapheme | Kind | Word |
|---------|-----------|------|------|
| /ee/    | ee        | base | seed |
| /ee/    | ea        | variant | leaf |
| /ee/    | e_e       | split digraph | theme |
| /ee/    | ey        | variant | key |
