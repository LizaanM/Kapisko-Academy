## Sessions

1. **Monday** — Meet the sound. Grapheme: `ee`. Blend: seed, sheep, green.
2. **Tuesday** — A different spelling. Sound variant: `ea` (leaf, bean, beach).
3. **Wednesday** — The magic e. Sound variant: `e_e` (these, theme, delete).
4. **Thursday** — Mix it up: word sort across all variants, mini story.
5. **Friday** — Show what you know: dictation, independent practice, star chart.
