# Kapisko Academy - Term 1 Curriculum Framework

**Alignment:** UK Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS)
**Duration:** 10 Weeks

## Implementation & Assessment Note

In accordance with EYFS statutory guidelines, progress tracking across the 17 Early Learning Goals (ELGs) relies on continuous, low-friction teacher observation and holistic profiling rather than formal testing. The Prime Areas form the continuous relational and developmental foundation woven into all daily activities.

## Term 1 Anchor Verses (NIV)

- **Home Language (Phonics):** Psalm 119:105 - "Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path."
- **Mathematics:** Luke 12:7 - "Indeed, the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Don't be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows."
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Proverbs 2:6 - "For the Lord gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding."
- **Life Skills (Biblical Theme):** Psalm 139:14 - "I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made; your works are wonderful, I know that full well."

---

## Daily Rhythmic Structure (Continuous Provision & Interleaved Flow)

- **08:15 – 08:30:** Welcome, Prayer, and Memory Verse Reading (Embedding Communication, Language, and PSED)
- **08:30 – 08:50:** Systematic Synthetic Phonics & Spaced Repetition Flashcards.
- **08:50 – 09:10:** Mathematics (Concrete manipulatives, subitising, and number bonds).
- **09:10 – 09:30:** Understanding the World (Biblical integration and thematic exploration).
- **09:30 – 10:00:** Snack & Outdoor Free Play (Gross motor development and social building relationships).
- **10:00 – 10:30:** Fine Motor Integration and Expressive Arts & Design.
- **10:30 – 11:15:** Logic, Critical Thinking, and Pattern Recognition.
- **11:15 – 12:15:** Afternoon Spaced Repetition and Flexible Project Time / Sensory Play.
- **12:15 – 12:30:** Pack up, Reflection, and Dismissal

## Weekly Progression

### Week 1

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _s_, _a_ (Phase 2 initial GPCs).
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Numbers 1 & 2 cardinality and subitising.
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (Biblical Theme):** Me / Created in God's Image.
- **Prime Area: PSED:** Identifying and understanding emotions.
- **Prime Area: Communication & Language:** Following simple instructions.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Attribute matching (categorising objects by colour and size).

### Week 2

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _t_, _i_.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Number 3 and spatial reasoning (above, below, next to).
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (Biblical Theme):** My Body / God Formed Adam and Eve.
- **Prime Area: Physical Development:** Fine motor practice and pencil grip introduction.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** 4-piece jigsaw puzzles.

### Week 3

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _p_, _n_.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Number 4 and 2D shapes (Circles & Squares).
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (Biblical Theme):** My Senses / Experiencing God's Creation.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Tangram basics (recreating simple physical silhouettes).

### Week 4

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _c_, _k_.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Deep dive into the composition of number 5 and comparing amounts.
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (Biblical Theme):** My Feelings (David's Psalms of emotion).
- **Prime Area: PSED:** Identifying and understanding emotions in oneself and others.
- **Prime Area: Physical Development:** Fine motor practice focussing on a dynamic tripod grip.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Pattern blocks for copying and extending visual sequences.

### Week 5

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _i2_, _e_.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Introduction of number bonds up to 5 and subitising.
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (Biblical Theme):** My Family (Noah's family works together).
- **Prime Area: PSED (Building Relationships):** Exploring positive family dynamics and cooperative play.
- **Prime Area: Communication and Language:** Audio-responsive speaking exercises using full sentences.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Sequencing cards for arranging 3-step logical story progressions.

### Week 6

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _h_, _r_.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Exploring odd and even numbers within 5, and sharing quantities equally.
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (Biblical Theme):** The Natural World (God created animals).
- **Prime Area: PSED (Managing Self):** Distinguishing right from wrong and understanding reasons for rules.
- **Prime Area: Physical Development (Gross Motor):** Negotiating space and obstacles safely.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Deductive ordering (arranging objects by multiple changing attributes).

### Week 7

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _m_, _d_.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Comparing mass and capacity using mathematical language.
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (The Natural World):** Exploring plants, seeds, and growth (Parable of the Sower).
- **Prime Area: PSED (Self-Regulation):** Controlling immediate impulses and delaying gratification.
- **Prime Area: Communication and Language:** Following complex instructions involving several actions.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Balance scale deduction (predicting heavier/lighter items).

### Week 8

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _g_, _o_.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Copying, continuing, and creating repeating patterns (ABAB, ABBABB).
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (Past and Present):** Learning about history through narratives (childhood of Jesus).
- **Prime Area: PSED (Managing Self):** Building resilience and perseverance in the face of a challenging task.
- **Prime Area: Physical Development (Fine Motor):** Focussing on using small tools safely (scissors and paintbrushes).
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Error detection in AB and ABB physical patterns.

### Week 9

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _u_, _l_.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Recognising 2D and 3D shapes, understanding spatial awareness, and using positional language.
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (The Natural World):** Exploring changing states of matter and weather (Jesus calming the storm).
- **Prime Area: Communication and Language:** Listening attentively and responding with relevant questions during non-fiction reading.
- **Prime Area: PSED (Managing Self):** Focussing attention on adult-led tasks even when engaged in self-directed play.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Picture Sudoku (using simple 3x3 grids with shapes).

### Week 10

- **Specific Area: Literacy (Phonics):** Sounds _f_, _y_, and consolidating blending skills for Phase 2.
- **Specific Area: Mathematics:** Term consolidation (numbers up to 5, cardinality, and subitising).
- **Specific Area: Understanding the World (Biblical Theme):** Term 1 Review (Thankfulness for God's creation).
- **Prime Area: PSED (Building Relationships):** Celebrating term achievements together and demonstrating sensitivity to others.
- **Prime Area: Physical Development (Gross Motor):** Energetic movement through running, jumping, and dancing.
- **Logic & Critical Thinking:** Basic mazes and physical multi-step rule games.
