# Kapisko Academy (A Not-for-Profit Organisation)

_An open-source Christian curriculum incorporating modern cognitive science methods for evidence-based learning._

## Core Ethos

1. **Biblical Foundation**: Grounding all learning in a Christian worldview, prioritising scripture memorisation and development of Christ-like character.
2. **Full Synthetic Phonics**: Strict decoding without reliance on sight words or picture cues.
3. **A Culture of Reading**: Strong encouragement of independent, extensive reading.
4. **Evidence-Based Retention**: Utilising spaced repetition, active retrieval practice, and interleaving for long-term mastery.
5. **Explicit Instruction & Mastery**: Presenting new material in small, manageable steps with worked examples, and requiring a high success rate (targeting 90% accuracy on independent practice) before progression.
6. **Critical Thinking & Problem Solving**: Integrated early logic and analytical skill development.
7. **Computer Skills**: Foundational digital literacy and technical proficiency.
8. **Rigorous Pathway Alignment**: Progressive mapping towards IGCSE/A-Level and US Diploma/SAT examinations.
9. **Collaborative Improvement**: An open-source model for continuous, expert peer-review and refinement.
10. **Free & Universal Access**: Providing every child with a free, evidence-based, high-quality learning experience, regardless of background or circumstance.
